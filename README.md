## About me

I'm a Software Developer and Information Systems undergraduate focused on building modern web and mobile applications.

I have experience developing mobile applications for the public sector, working on solutions involving real-world operational flows, geolocation, authentication, API integrations and communication with external services.

I've also worked on the development and maintenance of web platforms and websites for state government agencies, building interfaces, integrating backend services and implementing business rules tailored to institutional needs.

Beyond the public sector, I also work on commercial and independent projects, developing websites, business platforms and digital products.

Although my main focus is Web & Mobile Development, I'm also comfortable working across the backend, developing REST APIs, authentication systems, database integrations and business logic, mainly using PHP and Laravel.
